Stories written in stars

Long before phones, people used patterns of stars to tell time, find their way, and pass down stories.

Here are three you can spot from Texas.

The Hunter

Orion

Easiest to find in winter. The three stars in a tidy row are Orion’s Belt. The bright reddish shoulder, Betelgeuse, is a dying supergiant.

Ursa Major

Big Dipper

A scoop of seven stars. Follow the two stars at the end of the bowl and they point straight to Polaris, the North Star.

The Queen

Cassiopeia

A bright “W” (or “M”) that circles the North Star all year, so from Texas you can spot it on almost any clear night.
